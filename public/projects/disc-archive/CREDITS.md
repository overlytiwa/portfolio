# The Disc Archive project cover

The portfolio cover is a static editorial composition, made with SVG, using the album artwork supplied in the live Disc Archive project:

- The College Dropout: https://kw-seven.vercel.app/assets/covers/01.jpg
- Graduation: https://kw-seven.vercel.app/assets/covers/03.jpg

Album artwork belongs to its respective rights holders. The live project's disc labels are unofficial album-art concepts. This independent archive is unaffiliated with Kanye West or his labels.

The cover is a portfolio illustration of the project, rather than a screenshot of the WebGL interface.
