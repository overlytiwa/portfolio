# ADUNÉ HOME — Brand Strategy & Research

**Project:** Independent, speculative redesign of Aduné Home, prepared October 27, 2026.  
**Deliverables:** React website concept, brand system, brand book, print and packaging artwork, social templates, moodboard, mockups and this strategic rationale.  
**Status:** Concept only. Neither Aduné Home nor Fito Sauna has approved or commissioned the redesign.

## 01. Brand research: what is supported by public evidence

- **Existing identity:** ADUNÉ Home is a home-décor business whose public About page describes itself as celebrating craftsmanship, handwoven production and a blend of traditional techniques and contemporary aesthetics. Its stated values are elegance, craftsmanship and sustainability. Source: https://adunehome.com/about-us/
- **Existing range:** Home and living, dining, personal/everyday, art and fittings; observed listings include Aduné Palm Chair, The Fringe Placemat, Haven Tissue Storage Box, Aduné Lap Desk, Aduné Coaster Set and Ember Vase. Source: https://adunehome.com/shop/
- **Location and context:** The public homepage lists Abuja, Nigeria and a September 2025 launch. Source: https://adunehome.com/
- **Observed UX problems, not accusations:** The homepage/shop surfaced different currencies during research (EUR, USD, NGN in different contexts); currency handling should be audited. Homepage timeline copy says “31st of June,” an impossible date; it should be corrected or removed. Source: https://adunehome.com/ (accessed October 27, 2026).
- **No unsupported claims:** We have not independently verified supplier origins, handweaving techniques for every SKU, organic certification, manufacturing conditions or buyer demographics. The identity must not imply these are verified.

## 02. Inspiration audit: Fito Sauna

**What to learn from it:** The reference site uses an image-led hero, oversized emotional promise, extremely restrained navigation, spatial depth, tight section sequencing and modest scroll motion. Its creator's case study explicitly describes a product-led redesign, a layered hero, chunked content and restrained motion. Sources: https://fitosauna.com/ and https://theqream.com/case-study/fitosauna .

**What NOT to copy:** Logo, type design, particular layouts, sauna product story, original photography, award positioning and existing motion design. ADUNÉ instead uses cultural materiality, an editorial serif, a split narrative, botanical/letterform marks, a rotating visual rhythm, staggered product cards and room-led curation.

## 04. Proposed target audiences (hypotheses; validate with interviews/analytics)

**Primary / 27–42**: urban professionals, first-home renters and homeowners in Abuja/Lagos who invest selectively in interiors and follow design-led brands. Values: a home that feels personal, tactile quality, styling inspiration, clear pricing and reliable delivery. Pain points: generic décor, weak photos, uncertainty about size/material and online-store trust.

**Secondary / 25–45**: gift buyers seeking thoughtful wedding, housewarming and milestone gifts. Wants packaging that feels ready to give, gift-note option and unambiguous returns.

**Trade / 28–50**: stylists, interior designers and small hospitality businesses seeking cohesive accent pieces; need trade enquiries, stock and measurements.

**Suggested validation:** Five moderated customer interviews in each consumer segment, review of 90-day analytics, a product-card price/photography usability test, and a 10-order customer-service diary before treating the personas as facts.

## 05. Brand positioning

**Category:** Considered contemporary home objects.  
**Positioning statement:** *For design-aware people building spaces that feel personal, ADUNÉ offers expressive, approachable home objects with tactile character and a considered visual language, bringing warmth and meaning to everyday rituals.*  
**Reason to believe, pending verification:** The original brand claims craft-driven design and maintains a real collection of furniture, home and tabletop products. More specific artisan/manufacturing proof should be gathered.

**Difference:** Not generic trend-chasing décor; not an inaccessible museum aesthetic. Artful objects for use.  
**Promise:** The everyday can feel more like yours.  
**Pillars:** Feeling / Form / Craft / Everyday ritual.  
**Tagline:** **Objects of belonging.**  
**Alternate campaign lines:** “A little less ordinary. A lot more soul.” / “Beauty belongs in the everyday.” / “Not simply things to own. Things to live with.”

## 06. Naming, personality, and story

**Brand name:** **ADUNÉ HOME** is retained; this is a rebrand, not a rename. Use **aduné** as a lowercase editorial wordmark and **ADUNÉ HOME** in formal documentation. Do not assign an unverified linguistic etymology to the name.

**Personality:** Warm, curious, tactile, assured, cultured, quietly expressive.  
**Voice:** Human and sensory. Short, composed headlines; specific descriptions of forms, surfaces, use and styling. Avoid vague “luxury lifestyle” clichés or invented artisan stories.

**Proposed brand story:**

> A home is never made in a day. It forms through rituals, conversations and the objects we reach for without thinking. ADUNÉ celebrates those small acts of living with pieces that feel considered, expressive and quietly personal. Inspired by culture, drawn to texture and made for everyday moments, each object makes a space feel a little more like home.

This is **new creative copy**, not a verified founder history.

## 07. Creative direction and moodboard

**Idea:** “The lived-in gallery.” The warmth and imperfections of a real home meet the calm art direction of a design journal.

**Keywords:** tactile / sunlit / sculptural / woven / domestic / warm restraint / unhurried / collected.  
**Visual motifs:** an abstract A-and-leaf symbol (not a traditional cultural motif), interlaced line pattern and soft contour rings.  
**Compositions:** generous negative space, asymmetric alignment, overscale serif headlines, small editorial indexes and overlapping photography.  
**Motion:** layered hero push-in, 24px fade-up on scroll, slow monochromatic marquee, product-photo hover zoom, accessible reduced-motion fallback.  
**Photography:** late-afternoon directional light, imperfect linen, plaster, dark and mid-tone wood, close texture details. Feature product scale and a whole-room image for each product when original photography is available.

## 08. Brand color system

| Role | Name | Hex | Application |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Parchment | `#F0EDE5` | Page backgrounds, printed stationery |
| Contrast | Olive | `#4B5140` | Dark panels, packaging and buttons |
| Accent | Clay | `#AB7E64` | Small accents, tabs, editorial tags |
| Support | Sand | `#D6C8B6` | Soft panels, cards and background texture |
| Text | Ink | `#252820` | Headlines, icons and core copy |
| Light | Studio White | `#F8F6F0` | Clean contrast, product sections |

Primary pair: Parchment + Ink. Inverse: Olive + Studio White. Clay should not be used as small text on pale backgrounds without contrast review.

## 09. Typography and hierarchy

**Editorial serif:** Playfair Display Regular/Italic (Google Fonts); production fallback Georgia.  
**Functional sans:** DM Sans 400, 500, 600, 700 (Google Fonts); fallback Arial.  
**Logo treatment:** Custom typeset `aduné` with close tracking and an abstract A/leaf mark; vector files included. To finalize for commercial use, convert the selected wordmark lettering to outlined paths after typographic refinements.

| Style | Desktop | Mobile | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero display | 112–150px | 63–96px | Serif regular, negative tracking |
| Section display | 64–84px | 43–60px | Serif regular |
| Product title | 25px | 21px | Serif regular |
| Body | 15–16px | 12–14px | Sans, 1.6–1.9 line-height |
| Eyebrow / label | 9–10px | 8–9px | Sans medium, 0.12–0.20em tracking |

Spacing: 8px base system with editorial exceptions. Maintain min 16px for lengthy customer-service and commerce copy in production.

## 10. Application system

**Logo family:** Primary wordmark, reversed wordmark, secondary lockup, abstract A/leaf symbol and circular seal. Never stretch, tilt or apply a heavy shadow. Prefer 1.5× wordmark height of clear space.

**Patterns:** Interlaced lines and contour circles. Use as low-contrast secondary detail, not all-over wallpaper competing with products.

**Packaging:** kraft or natural-white bag, olive sleeve, paper tissue pattern, seal sticker and product tags. Suggested materials and dielines are illustrative until a supplier confirms specifications; don't present the mockups as production-approved.

**Labels:** Product name, material, product dimensions, care, maker country (only when verified), batch/order identifier and customer-service path. See editable template files.

**Printed pieces:** Front/back business cards and thank-you cards in editable SVG/PNG. Contact details are deliberately non-personal placeholders; site domain only.

**Social system:** Editorial brand story, object study, typographic quote, 9:16 collection story. Maintain generous margins and photograph-led messaging.

**Website:** Responsive React concept with home, collection, story, journal, product preview, category filters, wishlist-style saved drawer and animated reveals. No payment, inventory, account, email backend or analytics is connected.

## 11. Website audit → redesign choices

| Observed or likely opportunity | Proposed redesign response |
| --- | --- |
| Brand story competes with assorted shop categories | One clear proposition before the assortment: Objects of belonging |
| Product catalog lacks a unifying gallery presentation | Restrained cards, object-index system, room-led collections |
| Prices/currencies vary by site context | Use single selected display currency, explicit FX policy and checkout matching before launch |
| Product utility can get lost in aesthetic photography | Pair each lifestyle hero with true front/side/scale and dimensions on future product pages |
| Limited proof of origin/material | Add verified material, maker and care details, not invented badges |
| Motion inspiration could become heavy | CSS transforms + IntersectionObserver; `prefers-reduced-motion` supported |

## 12. Implementation and production notes

- The reference inspired **principles**, not a page-for-page copy.
- The concept photography in the included site comes from original, AI-generated art-direction collages cropped into editorial studies. It is **illustrative** and does **not** depict verified Aduné SKUs or brand-owned shoots.
- Mockups and templates use ADUNÉ, never a replacement brand. No trademark/rights transfer is implied.
- Site prices were derived from the Nigeria-facing shop page at research time and may change. All transactional links point to the original store, not a fabricated checkout.
- Review brand usage rights, logo distinctiveness/trademark clearance, true SKU imagery, measurements, materials, brand approvals, accessibility, checkout integration and supplier print specs before public use.

## 13. Success measures and next steps

**Recommended metrics:** product-list click-through; add-to-cart (only after commerce integration); contact-enquiry rate; page load and LCP; mobile category completion; checkout currency mismatch reports; return-related support tickets.

**Priority sequence:** 1) client validation + trademark screening, 2) product photo shoot and material fact-check, 3) integration with actual storefront + currency accuracy, 4) accessibility/performance testing, 5) production dielines and small print proof, 6) visual A/B test.

**Research date:** October 27, 2026. This document is a qualitative creative/research deliverable, not a statistically representative market survey.
