# ADUNÉ — Print & Packaging Concept Notes

The folder contains editable SVG masters and PNG proofs for:

- Shopping bag front, box sleeve, tissue art, seal sticker
- Product hangtag front/back, product information label template, packing insert
- Thank-you card front/back
- Business card front/back

**Production is not approved.** The drawings are presentation artworks, not final diecuts or bleed-ready artwork. Before print, request supplier dielines and confirm finished sizes, paper GSM, paper origin/certifications, inks, coating, folds, handles, bleed, safe zones, varnish and barcode requirements. Convert RGB color to an agreed CMYK print profile and order proofs.

Suggested print dimensions for discussion only:

| Item | Starting format to validate |
| --- | --- |
| Thank-you card | A6, 105 × 148 mm |
| Business card | 85 × 55 mm |
| Hang tag | 50 × 90 mm + hole |
| Product label | Size dictated by SKU and packaging |
| Bag | Match actual box/product dimensions, weight and handle load |

Fill material/origin/dimensions/care fields with **verified SKU data** only. Do not print placeholders, invented maker stories or unconfirmed environmental claims. Artwork has intentionally no personal contact details.
