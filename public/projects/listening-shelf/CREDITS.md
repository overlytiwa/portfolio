# The Listening Shelf portfolio assets

The cover is a manually composed layout of the project's existing sleeve artwork with original typography and arrangement. No image-generation tool was used.

Sleeve surfaces and the shelf screenshot come from [overlytiwa/the-listening-shelf](https://github.com/overlytiwa/the-listening-shelf). The cover includes The College Dropout, Yeezus, So Much Fun, Donda, Graduation, Who Really Cares, French Exit and Blonde.

Album artwork belongs to its respective artists, labels and rights holders. The source project's [artwork credits](https://github.com/overlytiwa/the-listening-shelf/blob/main/ARTWORK-SOURCES.md) identify its official catalogue sources.

The screenshot was resized/compressed for this portfolio. The case study is based on the published site, its album catalogue, the source implementation and the repository's shelf screenshot.
